HOW IT WORKS

Direction first. Production second.

01 · REQUEST

Tell me what the creative needs to accomplish and where it will be used.

02 · CONCEPT

Within roughly 1–2 business days, I develop the direction, sketch, script or shot plan.

03 · APPROVE

You get one revision of the vision/script before production. This is the moment to change the hat from blue to pink.

04 · PRODUCE

Production is scheduled and completed based on the project needs — often within the next several business days.

05 · EDIT

I finish the deliverables or coordinate the right editor/specialist.

06 · FINALIZE

One post-production revision is included for edits that can reasonably be made without recreating the project.

TIMING

Most focused projects: about 5–7 business days.

Simple projects may move much faster. Larger shoots or projects with outside vendors depend on schedules and scope. A new concept, new location, new wardrobe or new shoot after approval is a new Create scope — not an editing revision.
