MY SALES PHILOSOPHY

People buy
from people.

You can have a great product, beautiful branding, and a perfectly clear offer — and still lose the sale if the customer doesn’t trust you, connect with you, or feel understood.

When someone doesn’t buy, I usually look at three things:

01

THE PRICE

Does the value justify what you’re asking them to pay?

02

THE PRODUCT

Is this actually the right offer for the person you’re trying to reach?

03

THE PERSON

Have you created enough trust for someone to want to buy from you?

HOW I THINK

Start with the goal.
Then build around it.

I don’t want to create content simply because a business feels like it should be posting. I want to understand what we are trying to accomplish first.

Give me a business and a goal, and my mind immediately starts looking for the opportunity. Maybe the goal is selling a new product online. Maybe it’s filling appointments, introducing a business to a new neighborhood, creating a holiday promotion, or launching a service.

From there, we can build the website, campaign, messaging, creative assets, customer journey, or promotional plan that supports that goal.

Marketing shouldn’t begin with:

“What should we post?”

It should begin with:

“What are we trying to make happen?”

WHAT I BELIEVE

I don’t believe in marketing for the sake of looking busy.

A business doesn’t necessarily need thirty Reels. It doesn’t automatically need a complete rebrand. And it doesn’t need another generic campaign copied from everybody else in the industry.

Sometimes it needs one strong idea executed properly. A clear message. A thoughtful campaign. A better customer experience. A website that finally explains the offer. A few pieces of creative that give people a reason to care.
